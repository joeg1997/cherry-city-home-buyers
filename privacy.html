Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Cherry City Home Buyers LLC ("Cherry City Home Buyers," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share when visiting our website or submitting a property inquiry.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit our property consultation form or contact us directly, we may collect:

  • Contact details such as your full name, phone number, and email address;
  • Property details including street address, property type, condition notes, and preferred selling timeline;
  • Records of your communication preferences, including optional SMS/text message opt-in status.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Evaluate your property inquiry and prepare a no-obligation cash offer or consultation;
  • Communicate with you by phone, email, or (when consented) conversational text message regarding your inquiry;
  • Coordinate with licensed title and escrow companies if we mutually enter into a purchase agreement.

3. Mobile & SMS Privacy Protection (Strict Non-Sharing Policy)

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with, sold to, or rented to any third parties under any circumstances, except with telecommunications service providers strictly necessary to deliver messages you have requested.

4. Opting Out of Communications

You may opt out of receiving SMS messages from Cherry City Home Buyers LLC at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. For assistance, reply HELP. You may also request removal of your contact details at any time during a phone or email conversation with our team.

5. Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you submit against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
