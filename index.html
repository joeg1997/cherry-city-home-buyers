      Common Questions

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Clear answers about how selling directly to Cherry City Home Buyers works.

      How is Cherry City Home Buyers different from a real estate agent?

      Real estate agents list your property on the MLS, host showings, and charge roughly 6% in commissions if they find a buyer whose bank loan gets approved. We are direct property buyers—we purchase your house directly (and/or with our investment partners), charge zero commissions, and let you skip repairs and showings completely.

      Am I under any obligation if I enter my address?

      Zero obligation whatsoever. Submitting your property details simply lets us research your property, have a quick conversation about your goals, and present a written offer. Whether you accept, take time to think, or walk away is 100% up to you.

      Do I really not have to fix or clean anything?

      Not a thing. You don’t need to paint, patch drywall, replace appliances, haul off old furniture, or even sweep the floors. Take what you want to keep and leave the rest behind.

      How fast can we actually close?

      Once we agree on terms and open escrow with a licensed title company, we can close in as little as 7 to 14 days—or set the closing date 30, 60, or 90 days out if you want extra time to organize your move.

      What if my property has tenants, liens, or major repairs?

      We regularly purchase tenant-occupied rentals, inherited homes in probate, and houses needing major structural or cosmetic work. Standard title payoffs and municipal liens are coordinated directly through escrow at closing.
